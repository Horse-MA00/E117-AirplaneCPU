# Customer scenarios and LLM adapter

The current engine is shared-market-v3 with fixed D28/D14/D7 additive rounds; read [the release rule](../docs/THREE_ROUND_RELEASE.md). The shared-market flow connects `market_adapter.py` and `market_experiment.py` to `backend/shared_market.py`. Travellers compete for the same inventory, retain budgets and memories across several trips, and change a single accounting ledger. The deterministic and real-provider modes receive the same public observations. See [the shared-market design](../docs/SHARED_MARKET_AGENT.md).

The earlier flow remains available through `runner.run(..., scenario_mode="journey")`. It follows subscription, D28/D14/D7 quotes, a plan change and end-of-term intention, with durable raw request/response audit. See [the full scenario design](../docs/LLM_SCENARIO_DESIGN.md), `journey.py`, `journey_runner.py`, `audit.py` and the resumable `experiment.py` CLI. Its individual worlds have independent inventory, so they are not a market-clearing experiment. The original two-stage mode remains as `scenario_mode="legacy"` for compatibility.

New journey (`traveller-journey-v2.2-en`) and shared-market (`shared-market-customer-v3.1-en`) explanations are English-only, with amounts denominated in HKD. Traveller language remains background context. `output_contract.py` checks scripts, common foreign currency labels and reason codes before acceptance; failures retain raw audit evidence. This is not a complete semantic language/arithmetic verifier. New suites and cache identities isolate this contract from historical multilingual outputs. A fresh five-person/six-stage DeepSeek check produced **30 English responses, 0 cache hits and 0 failures** in [english_journey_20261114](experiments/english_journey_20261114/).

Fourteen archetypes cover travel purpose, frequency, budget, flexibility, group size, booking notice, route fit and languages. Their weights are **assumptions**, not verified HK Express market shares. Coverage is not statistical representativeness.

`population.py` creates persistent IDs. `prompts/customer_system.txt` defines bounded, self-interested choices. `runner.py` validates actions and budget, labels deterministic versus live responses, and never silently replaces a failed LLM call. There is no passenger personal data.

Live mode requires server-only `LLM_API_URL` (an HTTPS Chat Completions endpoint), `LLM_API_KEY`, `LLM_MODEL`. `deepseek-flash` completed 672 validated decisions across the earlier 112-world factorial experiment, plus 60 fresh shared-prompt behavior probes. Raw redacted requests and responses are in `experiments/`. The UI limits live batches; the experiment CLIs have explicit call limits. Cached responses retain their original provider source and are not counted as new calls. A forced legal action is executed by the environment and never counted as an independent model choice. Neither mode reserves real airline inventory.

The pricing optimizer uses the repeatable mathematical market; changing prompts does not change its evaluation world. Agent outputs are hypotheses, not purchase ground truth. Calibrate future versions against consented interviews, priced choice experiments, and a real trial. Do not infer individual preferences from protected demographic attributes.

The 60 behavioral probes found three repeated-input groups with inconsistent actions, even with temperature zero. No observed order/language flips or upward-price membership reversals in this small selected suite establish reliable demand elasticity. Keep these diagnostics separate from policy profitability and empirical validation.

The historical shared-market-v2 validation used three predeclared held-out seeds and nine policy worlds: **1,383 fresh DeepSeek choices, zero cache/failures, and 1,390 environment-forced transitions**. Under the stated costs, `NO_OFFER` had the highest contribution in all three seeds; the candidate's mean increment was −HKD2,626.33. Removing only the assumed HKD1,500 program overhead changes the fixed policy's mean increment to +HKD328, so conclusions remain conditional on costs. See [the validation report](../docs/SHARED_MARKET_VALIDATION.md), public raw records in `experiments/shared_validation_*/`, and the explicitly retrospective [calibration provenance attestation](../docs/SHARED_MARKET_CALIBRATION_ATTESTATION.json).

The new three-round policy has a separate40-seed/120-case deterministic regression in `experiments/three_round_40seeds/`. Historical live outputs remain unchanged and are not new-policy validation.
