What this experiment measures

Revenue includes normal and member base-ticket sales in the selected flight’s simulated future. This accounts for normal-price sales displaced by a cheaper offer. It excludes membership fees, surcharges, ancillary revenue and all costs.

All panel travellers receive free eligibility; one seat per party can use the member fare, while companions pay the normal fare. Dates, budgets, cancellations, shared inventory and D28 / D14 / D7 release rules still apply.

The optimizer chooses what to test; the environment calculates the result. Generated traveller feedback is an assumption, not real HK Express demand. The result is the best tested candidate under this experiment, not a proven global optimum.
