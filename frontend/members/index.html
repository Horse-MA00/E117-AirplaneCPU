The pass covers one person. Tickets and mandatory fees are paid separately. Seats and dates are subject to availability.

Know what your member fare covers

Personal access

One member, one seat per booking. Companion and group bookings are not supported here.

Transparent payment

Tickets and mandatory fees are separate from the one-time membership fee. Existing active members do not pay the membership fee again.

Extras and changes

Baggage and seat selection are not sold here. No included checked bag is promised. Changes, cancellations and renewal are not available in this booking prototype.

Released seats only; availability is not guaranteed. No real payment is collected and no airline ticket is issued.
