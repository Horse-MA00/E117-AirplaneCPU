Publishing rules

One publication per D28, D14 or D7 round. Valid unsold quota from earlier rounds is deducted before new seats are issued.

Enter the fare yourself after reviewing the Thompson result. The server checks the 80% fare cap, whole-HKD pricing and the current protection again when you publish, and every member booking is checked again on the member site.

Members can book these seats as soon as you publish.
