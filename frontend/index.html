    How the scenario works and what it can tell you

    Each traveller has an independent example world. Their budget covers one trip for the whole party and any new membership fee. Expected trips over the 365-day term inform the membership decision; those future trips are not purchases in this ledger.

    The scripted D28 / D14 / D7 checkpoints have 4 / 0 / 1 member seats. Normal ticket totals are HK$850 / HK$900 / HK$1,050 before required baggage; a member ticket is the chosen base fare plus HK$250. Baggage is HK$80 per checked bag. Only the holder gets the member fare, companions pay regular fares.

    At D3, changed plans may cause cancellation under the stated refund rules. The final stage records renewal intention without another payment. Already-booked travellers do not buy twice. A sole legal action is executed by the environment, not counted as a new model choice.

    These are fixed test conditions, not NHPP allocations. Use Test the market for competing travellers and D28 / D14 / D7 quota carry-over. Population weights are assumptions; a larger panel is not proof of actual HK Express demand.
