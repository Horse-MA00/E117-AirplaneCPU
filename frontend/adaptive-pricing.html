Customer mix and run settings

New experiments use eight groups of flexible bargain travellers. These are test-population weights, not measured HK Express market shares. Positive groups receive at least one test member; reporting corrects for their sampling proportions.

Total: —

Parallel calls collect purchase intentions. The market then clears the shared inventory in a fixed order.

New fares use whole HKD. Each round compares the candidate and initial fare using the same new people in separate scenarios. Personal budgets are hidden from the price learner.

No membership charge is included. Taxes, fuel surcharges and operating costs are outside this experiment.
