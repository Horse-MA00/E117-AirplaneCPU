# NHPP and pricing implementation progress

2026-11-14: Implemented `backend/nhpp.py` and `backend/pricing.py` with no LightGBM dependency. The NHPP uses as-of gross counts, exact interval exposure, a beta-grid posterior and Gamma rate uncertainty. Negative beta is allowed. Capacity-hit intervals are excluded and heavy censoring or an abrupt surge prevents member release.

The pricing search evaluates 192 full-term candidate policies against identity-paired ordinary sales. Fees are counted once, costs include every accepted trip, quotas and total capacity are enforced, and service constraints prevent charging for an unusable programme. Holdout seeds and cost/interest/demand stress scenarios are reported separately from selection. All customer behaviour parameters remain explicit synthetic assumptions.

Eleven model tests pass at `backend/tests/test_models.py`, covering as-of leakage, negative beta, cancellation separation, cutoff, calibration separation, financial identities, inventory conservation, no-supply behaviour, chronological decisions, same-day itinerary deduplication and consistent aggregate explanations. The annual flight catalogue is deliberately a finite scenario; it is not a claim to reproduce HK Express actual market share, schedules or price elasticity.

Integration review corrected the exponential late-tail protection integral, restricted events and opening dates to the actual as-of horizon, aligned membership value with ex-ante expected customer opportunities, and added a compound-count/correlated-timing group-demand stress case. Membership simulation remains individual purchase acceptance; it does not enforce all-or-nothing group fulfilment.

Initial synthetic evaluation found nominal raw P90 coverage below 90%, especially under unknown future surges. A separate calibration split now supplies an additive protection allowance through `simulation/generated/calibration.json`; the data evaluation module evaluates disjoint flight IDs. `raw_protection_p90` preserves the unadjusted quantile; `protection_p90` includes the allowance. Means and raw forecast bands are unchanged. No parameters were tuned to hide the original evaluation result, and the allowance is calibrated for D28 only.

Default full-term search on the first ten generated backgrounds ran in approximately 0.14–0.43 seconds each before calibration, including both OFFER and NO_OFFER outcomes. Those simulated profits are not airline performance claims. Final evaluation files are owned by the data/evaluation module.

Final cross-module review found that a 30-day term compresses multiple synthetic departures into one calendar day. The market now assigns at most one exogenous itinerary per customer/calendar day, before both baseline and offer simulations, and bounds expected trip opportunities by available departure days. Aggregate feasibility explanations now reflect all replicates, not only the first result. Nine separate mocked-provider tests cover live-mode two-stage decisions, HTTP response parsing/cache replay, no-redirect enforcement, and prevention of fallback/credential disclosure. All 25 backend tests pass after integration with store tests; actual live-provider calls remain dependent on configured credentials.

2026-11-14 scoped pricing v3 correction: The completed v2 experiment on SYN-00004 had zero released member seats at D28/D14/D7. Its HK$110 average contribution difference came from one household cancelling in one policy world but flying in another: HK$100 less cancellation income plus HK$120 additional passenger cost in one of two replicas. Normal base-fare revenue did not change. This is not evidence of member-fare elasticity. The historical run and provider outputs remain unchanged.

New scoped runs now check the selected release day's NHPP headroom before creating a run or making provider calls. Zero headroom, or a release fraction that rounds allocation to zero, gives an actionable refusal. Read-only initial-policy suggestions still return the snapshot, zero supply and reason. The check says nothing about later D14/D7 availability; each selected round is evaluated separately. `scope.optimization_eligible`, `optimization_unavailable_reason` and `member_supply_exposure` expose this contract.

The new `flight-policy-feedback-v3` protocol fixes the seat-feedback profile throughout the member-fare search and its independent holdout. `next_seat_calibration` / `proposed_next_seat_calibration` record feedback proposals, explicitly not applied during the comparison. This deliberately separates the effects of price and changing seat protection; it is a proposed subsequent-experiment update, not a claim that both models are learning simultaneously. Annual fee remains fixed and one third is attributed to this one-flight experiment. Existing legacy multi-flight runs retain their prior search behavior and archived v2 reports retain their original version.

Scoped price probes start around the dataset's P0 using a 25% relative step: P0=HK$73.50 tests HK$55.12 and HK$91.88 first, followed by explicitly labelled lower-bound and wider probes. A zero P0 uses 5% of the configured interval as its local step. Candidates already tested are immutable; the finite search does not certify a global optimum.

Reports now separate accepted member/regular household buyers and tickets, new pass joins, fulfilled buyers/tickets/bookings, cancellations, released member seats, and actual member-offer observations. Member and regular buyer groups can overlap when companions pay regular fares or a household rebooks; background customers are excluded from buyer counts. Ledger-derived `profit_components`, `profit_attribution` (agent/background/program) and paired component deltas explain where contribution changes originate. Paired variance and standard error remain visible; synthetic LLM variability is not interpreted as observed customer demand.

`price_evidence_status` distinguishes `uninformative_no_supply`, `uninformative_no_member_exposure`, `no_member_sales` and `observed_member_sales`. No-supply or no-exposure rows cannot become retained best prices; all-zero released supply ends that track without a false holdout recommendation. Actual exposed offers with zero purchases remain visible as zero-sales observations, but cannot satisfy the member-service feasibility rule. Eighteen focused optimization tests passed, including initially failing regressions for zero-supply gating, local probes, buyer overlap, cancellation accounting, fixed seat profiles, no-supply termination, and bounded mocked-provider batching. No paid provider call, Docker restart, frozen NHPP/calibration edit or historical-result mutation was performed for this correction.
